# percinel — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 6 July 2026**

percinel ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal movie and series diary for Android. This policy explains what the app does and doesn't do with your information. The short version: **your data is yours, it lives on your device, and we never see it.**

---

## The short version

- percinel has **no servers of its own** and **no accounts** to create.
- Everything you log stays **on your phone** by default.
- If you choose to turn on backup, your data is stored in **your own Google Drive** — we can't see it or access it.
- We run **no analytics, no ads, no trackers**, and we **never sell or share** your data.

---

## What information percinel handles

### 1. The watches you create
When you use percinel you create entries — movie/series titles, your ratings, notes, and the date and time you watched something — plus an optional profile name. This information is stored **locally on your device** in the app's private storage. It never leaves your device unless *you* turn on cloud backup (below).

### 2. Optional cloud backup (Google Drive)
Backup and sync are **off by default**. If you turn them on:

- The app asks your permission to access a **private, app-specific folder in your own Google Drive** (the `appDataFolder`). This is a hidden area that only percinel can use — it is not visible in your normal Drive files, and other apps cannot read it.
- percinel writes a single backup file of your watches to that folder so your data can be restored or synced across your own devices.
- **We do not operate any server and never receive, see, or store this data.** It travels directly between your device and your Google Drive. Your backup is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- percinel only ever requests the `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata` permission. It **cannot** see, read, or modify any other file in your Google Drive.

### 3. Movie and series information (TMDB)
When you search for a title, your search text is sent to **The Movie Database (TMDB)** to return matching titles, posters, and details. This is necessary for the app to function. TMDB's handling of that request is governed by [TMDB's Privacy Policy](https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy). percinel does not send TMDB any of your personal watches, ratings, or notes.

---

## Google user data — Limited Use disclosure

percinel's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the **Limited Use** requirements. Specifically:

- We access only your Google Drive `appDataFolder`, solely to back up and restore your own percinel data at your request.
- We do **not** transfer this data to any third party, use it for advertising, or allow humans to read it.
- We do **not** use this data for any purpose other than providing the backup and sync feature you turned on.

---

## What we do *not* collect

- No analytics or usage tracking.
- No advertising or advertising identifiers.
- No location data.
- No contacts, photos, or files beyond the private backup folder described above.
- No accounts, emails, or passwords stored by us.

---

## Permissions the app uses

- **Internet** — to search TMDB for titles and posters, and (only if you enable it) to back up to your Google Drive.

---

## Keeping and deleting your data

- **On your device:** delete individual watches any time, or use **Settings → Clear everything** to erase all local data.
- **Cloud backup:** use **Settings → Turn off sync** to stop backing up. To remove the backup itself, you can delete the percinel app-data from your Google Account at [Google's "Apps with access to your data" / Drive app-data controls](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
- Uninstalling the app removes all local data from your device.

---

## Children's privacy

percinel is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
